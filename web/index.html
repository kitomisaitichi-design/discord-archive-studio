Connection limits & archive recovery

Personal-session automation is unofficial and conflicts with Discord's account-automation policy. Pacing and current-access checks reduce unnecessary requests; they cannot guarantee protection from account enforcement.

Missing channels are quarantined, their schedules are disabled, and saved messages remain available offline. Restore access before explicitly retrying. Previous deletions and unseen edit history cannot be recovered.
